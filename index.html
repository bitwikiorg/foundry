Submission agreement

Review the request

Submitting is the agreement. There is no separate consent checkbox.

Public output. If accepted, Foundry may analyze, summarize, visualize, cache, and publicly publish information derived from the submitted public repository.
Admission is reviewed. Submission does not guarantee indexing. Foundry may reject, defer, or pause work based on safety, technical fit, usefulness, size, and capacity.
No secrets or restricted code. Do not submit private, confidential, credential-bearing, or access-restricted repositories.
Email and updates. We may email repository status, Foundry/BITwiki product updates, and occasional newsletters. You can unsubscribe at any time.
Selecting Agree & queue repository submits the request under these terms.
